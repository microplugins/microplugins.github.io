General & The Micro Philosophy

Most plugins try to become massive software suites with 50 different settings tabs, third-party analytics tracking, and upsell popups in your WordPress admin. MicroPlugins are designed with surgical single-purpose responsibility: under 50KB code footprint, zero database query overhead, and no dashboard advertisements.

Compatibility & Performance

All our plugins require WordPress 6.0 or higher and PHP 8.0 or higher. We actively test and maintain full compatibility with PHP 8.1, 8.2, and 8.3, ensuring modern type safety and strict error handling.
