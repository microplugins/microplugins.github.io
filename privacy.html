Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Overview & Philosophy

At MicroPlugins ("we", "us", or "our"), privacy is a core architectural value, not an afterthought. We believe software should respect user boundaries, minimize data collection, and operate with full transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines how we treat information when you visit our website (microplugins.com) and when you use our WordPress plugins.

2. Plugin Telemetry & Data Collection

Plugin Runtime: Our WordPress plugins (including Ultimate Media Player and Ultimate Popup Maker) do not collect, store, or transmit personal data or analytics telemetry to our servers. All processing occurs locally on your WordPress host.

Pro License Activations: If you purchase a Pro license, our software sends an encrypted API request to our licensing endpoint solely to verify your license status and deliver plugin updates. This request includes only your license key, WordPress core version, and active site URL.

3. Website Information We Collect

When you visit our website or contact us:

  • Contact Information: If you submit a contact form or purchase a license, we collect your name and email address to provide customer support and send invoice receipts.
  • Payment Information: All payment transactions are processed directly by our third-party payment processors (Stripe or Paddle). We never store credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Server Logs: Like standard web hosts, our server infrastructure temporarily logs incoming IP addresses, browser user-agents, and requested URLs for security defense against DDoS attacks. Logs are automatically purged after 30 days.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website does not use invasive third-party marketing trackers or ad-retargeting pixels. Essential cookies are used exclusively for shopping cart session management and user authentication.

5. Data Retention & Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your account. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@microplugins.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our legal obligations or software features. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
