Refund Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

??? 14-Day 100% Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to build with complete confidence. If any MicroPlugins software does not meet your performance, design, or workflow requirements, you are entitled to a full 100% refund within 14 days of your initial purchase date?no hassle, no friction.

1. Scope & Eligibility

This Refund Policy applies to all digital software licenses, add-ons, and bundles purchased directly through MicroPlugins.com, including:

  • Ultimate Media Player and Playlist (Single Site, Agency Unlimited, or Lifetime License)
  • Ultimate Popup Maker (Single Site, Agency Unlimited, or Lifetime License)
  • Agency All-Access Bundle (Annual or Lifetime License)

To be eligible for a full refund, your refund request must be submitted within 14 calendar days from the exact timestamp of your initial transaction.

2. Eligible Refund Circumstances

We strive to provide modular, lightweight, high-performance WordPress code that adheres strictly to WordPress coding standards. You are eligible for a complete refund in circumstances including:

  • Technical Incompatibility: The plugin encounters an unresolvable conflict with your WordPress environment, host architecture, or active plugins, and our support team is unable to provide a fix or workaround.
  • Missing Workflow Feature: You discover that the plugin lacks a specific capability or integration necessary for your project that was reasonably assumed based on our documentation or feature descriptions.
  • Accidental Duplicate Purchases: You inadvertently purchased multiple licenses for the same product or selected the incorrect plan tier.
  • Unmet Performance Expectations: The software does not deliver the lightweight footprint or performance improvements you anticipated for your production environment.

3. Non-Refundable Conditions

While our policy is generous, refunds cannot be granted in the following scenarios:

  • Requests Made After 14 Days: Any refund request received after the 14-day window from the original purchase date is strictly ineligible.
  • Failure to Cooperate with Basic Diagnostics: If you request a refund citing technical failure or fatal bugs, but decline to provide diagnostic logs, allow our technical support engineers to inspect the issue, or test standard WordPress conflict isolation steps.
  • Hosting or Server Restrictions: Issues caused exclusively by non-standard hosting limitations that violate WordPress minimum requirements (such as obsolete PHP versions below 7.4, disabled standard PHP extensions, or restrictive hosting firewall blocks on BunnyStream APIs).
  • Unilateral Chargebacks or Disputes: Opening a dispute or chargeback with your bank or credit card provider without contacting our support team first. Initiating a bank dispute constitutes a breach of our Terms of Service, immediately terminates your license keys, and permanently blacklists your account from future purchases.

4. Annual Subscription Renewals

Annual licenses renew automatically each year to ensure uninterrupted access to security patches, compatibility updates for new WordPress major releases, and technical support.

We send an email notification prior to your automated renewal date. If you forgot to cancel your renewal before it processed, you may request a courtesy refund within 7 calendar days of the renewal charge. Renewal refunds requested after 7 days will not be approved, but you may cancel subsequent renewals at any time via your account dashboard.

5. How to Request a Refund

Requesting a refund is simple and straightforward:

  1. Contact Us: Submit a request via our Contact Support Form or email our billing desk directly at support@microplugins.com.
  2. Provide Transaction Details: Include your purchase email address, order invoice number, and the license key associated with the purchase.
  3. Share Optional Feedback: While not mandatory, briefly letting us know why the product didn't fit your needs helps our engineering team refine future releases.
  4. Fast Processing: Our billing team will review and process your refund within 24 to 48 business hours. Funds will be returned via your original payment method (Stripe or PayPal). Depending on your bank, credit card refunds typically reflect in your account within 3?7 business days.

6. Post-Refund License Status

Upon issuance of a refund:

  • Your Pro license key will be permanently deactivated.
  • Access to automatic updates, new feature releases, and dedicated priority support will be terminated immediately.
  • Access to proprietary cloud API gateways (e.g., automated BunnyStream transcoding pipelines or advanced telemetry webhooks) will cease.
  • In accordance with GNU General Public License (GPL) terms, any code already installed on your local environments remains open source, but running unmaintained, outdated versions on production sites is strongly discouraged for security reasons.
