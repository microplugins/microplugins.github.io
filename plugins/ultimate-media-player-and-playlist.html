Enterprise Media Features

Built for Performance, Security & Conversion

Everything course platforms, premium podcasters, and high-traffic video sites need to deliver studio-quality media.

Anti-Piracy Dynamic Watermarking

Deter illegal screen recording and course leaks by dynamically floating and jumping the viewer's username, email, IP address, and date across the video frame.

Bunny.net Stream & Adaptive HLS

Deliver lightning-fast buffer-free streaming worldwide with automatic resolution ladder switching (1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p) based on viewer connection speed.

In-Video Lead Generation Gate

Pause video playback at any designated timestamp and require viewers to submit their email address to continue. Automatically sync leads to your CRM via webhooks.

Cookieless Privacy-First Analytics

Track completion rates, average watch durations, drop-off curves, and engagement without intrusive cookie consent banners. 100% GDPR and CCPA compliant.

Interactive Chapters & Markers

Organize long masterclasses or podcast episodes with clickable timestamp chapters. Automatically generates Google VideoObject schema for rich search snippets.

Multi-Track Playlists & Audio Pods

Create seamless multi-episode video courses, album audio players, or curated video channels with search, auto-play next, and shuffle support.

Installation & Setup

WordPress Admin Upload

  1. Navigate to Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin.
  2. Select the ultimate-media-player-and-playlist.zip file.
  3. Click Install Now and activate the plugin.
  4. Go to Media Player → Settings to set branding colors, Bunny.net credentials, and watermark rules.

Adding to Pages & Posts

Open the Gutenberg editor and insert the Ultimate Media Player or Media Playlist block. Configure source URLs, poster thumbnails, and chapters directly in the block inspector sidebar.

Shortcode Alternative 
[mump_player id="12"]
[mump_playlist id="series-1"]
